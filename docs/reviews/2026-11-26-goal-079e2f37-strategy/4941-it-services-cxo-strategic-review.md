# #4941 — IT services CxO strategic review

## Question

Assess Devin and Synlynk as separate client-engagement trials while preserving an IT services firm’s existing delivery SOPs.

## Verdict

**Derived from the recorded synthesis.** Trial Devin for enterprise modernization, managed services, and accompanied staff augmentation; limit Synlynk to a supervised dedicated pod or regulated local-model pilot. The synthesis does not support multi-customer Synlynk deployment until execution gates, authoritative state reconciliation, and tenant isolation exist.
