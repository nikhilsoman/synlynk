## 1. Executive Summary & Verdict

### **Verdict: APPROVED (Production Ready)**
PR #2628 delivers the complete work-start lease enforcement and conflict prevention architecture required for v0.26.3. It successfully solves the long-standing cross-harness collision and double-dispatch risk by enforcing mutual exclusion at the exact boundary where tasks transition into execution.

### **PR Quality Score: 9.6 / 10**

### **Core Capabilities Reviewed & Verified:**
1. **Mandatory Lease Acquisition at Work-Start:** Transitions via `synlynk story update <story_id> --status in_progress` now atomically acquire a rolling task lease in SQLite `task_leases` and record the claim in active session tracking. Conflicting active claims are refused with exit code `1` unless overridden.
2. **Pre-Dispatch Story Lease Gate:** Headless `synlynk dispatch --story <id>` verifies whether an active, unexpired, live-PID interactive home session holds the target story. Conflicting dispatches are blocked immediately with `RuntimeError` unless `--override` / `--force` is specified.
3. **Dispatch Worker Lease Accounting:** Upon successful agent process spawn, background dispatch jobs automatically record a worker lease (`dispatch:<agent>:<job_id>`) in `task_leases`, eliminating double-dispatch races across autonomous cycles.
4. **Self-Healing Lease Diagnostic & Auto-Remediation:** Integrated `_hc_task_leases_integrity` into `synlynk doctor` and added `synlynk doctor --fix leases` to automatically identify dead-PID and expired leases and revert unworked stories safely back to `ready`.
5. **Schema & Database Resilience:** `_ensure_task_leases_columns` guarantees backward-compatible SQLite migration for `pid` and `harness` columns and fail-open logging on transient database lock contentions.

## 2. In-Depth Architectural & Code Analysis

### 2.1 State Transitions & CLI (`synlynk/db.py`, `synlynk/cli.py`, `synlynk/story_claims.py`)
- **Claim Binding:** `cmd_story_update` cleanly differentiates status transitions. Setting `--status in_progress` acquires a task lease with session identifier `{harness}:{hostname}:{pid}`, ensuring clear provenance.
- **Lease Release on Completion/Revert:** Transitioning to `--status ready` or `--status draft` automatically releases active leases in `task_leases` and removes the claim marker from `active_claims.json`. Setting `--status done` delegates to `cmd_story_done` to cleanly archive the story.
- **Metadata Mutators:** Full support for updating `--title`, `--stage`, `--role`, and `--phase` allows seamless story maintenance without losing or corrupting lease state.

### 2.2 Dispatch Gate & Conflict Prevention (`synlynk/dispatch.py`)
- **Scope Precision:** `_verify_dispatch_story_lease` ignores ad-hoc stories (`story-adhoc-*`) and empty IDs, avoiding unnecessary overhead for ephemeral micro-tasks.
- **Worker Isolation:** The gate distinguishes between interactive home sessions and background dispatch workers (`is_dispatch_worker = leased_by.startswith("dispatch:")`), ensuring fleet workers do not inadvertently lock out legitimate subtask dispatches while still preventing home/headless collisions.
- **Preemption Control:** The `--override` flag cleanly expires stale conflicting leases and reassigns work when a user or conductor explicitly mandates takeover.

### 2.3 Doctor Diagnostics & Recovery (`synlynk/doctor.py`)
- **Process Liveness Verification:** Leverages `synlynk.jobs._pid_is_alive` to distinguish between genuinely active running processes and stranded dead PIDs (e.g. from aborted terminals or killed jobs).
- **Safe Story Reversion:** During `doctor --fix leases`, stories are only reverted to `ready` if no active daemon workers (`daemon_jobs`) and no other active task leases remain, preventing race conditions with concurrent active jobs.
